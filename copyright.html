3. Copyright Complaints

If you believe that your copyrighted work is being unlawfully used in connection with Nuvexa, you may contact us with details of the alleged infringement.

A copyright complaint should contain enough information for us to understand and investigate the claim.

4. Information to Include

Where applicable, a copyright complaint should include:

  • Your full name and contact information.
  • A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  • The URL or other information identifying the relevant content or service location.
  • An explanation of why you believe the use is unauthorized.
  • A statement confirming that the information provided is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
  • Any additional information reasonably necessary to investigate the complaint.

We may review copyright complaints and take appropriate action where we determine that action is warranted and legally appropriate.

Depending on the circumstances, action may include restricting access to functionality, removing or disabling relevant material under our control, or contacting the relevant parties.

Copyright owners may also need to contact the relevant third-party platform directly regarding content hosted or controlled by that platform.

Copyright complaints should be made in good faith and should contain accurate information.
